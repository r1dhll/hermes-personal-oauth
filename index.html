Hermes Personal

Hermes Personal is a private desktop agent for Ridhul Vidhul. It runs on his Mac (Hermes by Nous Research) so he can ask the agent about his own Gmail and Google Calendar, and related Google Workspace files, without doing that work by hand.

What it does

  • Read and send mail in Ridhul’s Gmail when he asks
  • List, create, and update events on his Google Calendar when he asks
  • Optionally read or write Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts if those permissions stay enabled

This is not a public product. It is not listed on an app store. Only Ridhul signs in with his Google account.
